## The idea

**Each service page becomes the public version of your proposals.** Your proposals make people want this. The pages only describe it. The selling is already written, so most of this job is moving it onto the site with client names and figures taken out.

## What's wrong today

- **All eight pages are the same template:** title, one paragraph, four cards, questions, button. Only the words change.
- **The biggest section is about us.** "How we build it" (Immerse, Centralise, Build, Compound) tells a buyer our process, not what they get.
- **The page never says what the thing does.** The list of what each service does exists, but the page doesn't show it. It only appears on the overview page.
- **Nothing is shown.** No screen, no picture of the work, no today against tomorrow.
- **The value is missing.** Nothing on capacity, time back or what happens to the business. The four smaller pages have one paragraph and one question each.
- **Your best selling lines appear nowhere:** "Your team only has to check it", "We run it, always", "Admin stops being the ceiling".

## Command Centre, written out in full

This is the sample to react to. If this feels right, the other seven follow it.

**The promise.** See everything. Approve what matters. From your phone.
Your own app, on your phone and at your desk, where your people and your agents run the business together. Every number live, every job moving, and your team only has to check the work.

**Standing still.** Today, your business lives in a dozen places.
- Every update means phoning someone and waiting.
- Every report gets rebuilt by hand, from five spreadsheets.
- Every problem shows up at month end, not the week it happened.
- Every new customer brings more admin, and sooner or later the next hire.

**What it does.**
- **Your whole business, on one screen.** Cash, jobs, sales and what's late, summary first and detail one click away. You no longer have to go looking.
- **Ask it anything.** Type or speak a question and your CEO Agent answers with the figure and the working behind it. Nobody has to phone anyone.
- **Your agents, in plain view.** What each one has finished, what it's on now and what's waiting for you.
- **Approve in one click.** Anything high-impact comes to the right person first, on their phone or at their desk.
- **A login for each of your team.** Everyone sees and approves only what their role allows.
- **Every action recorded,** with the reason and the documents behind it.

**Day to day.**

| When | Today | With your Command Centre |
|---|---|---|
| Every morning | You go looking for problems | What's due and what's late is waiting for you |
| You need a number | You phone someone and wait for a spreadsheet | You ask out loud and get the figure in seconds, with its working |
| Month end | The report is rebuilt by hand | It's drafted with the working shown. You only have to check it |
| Something needs sign-off | It sits in an inbox until someone chases it | It lands on your phone. You approve it in a click |
| Someone is off or leaves | What they know goes with them | It's written down as the work happens, and it stays yours |

**The picture.** It's Monday, 9am. Before you've opened your laptop, last week's cash is on your phone, three approvals are waiting and a late payment has already been chased. You tap twice and start your day on the work only you can do.

**What you get back.**
**More work, same team.** Admin stops being the ceiling, so how far you scale is set by the work you win, not the people you can hire.
- **Time.** Your best people back on the work only they can do.
- **Speed.** Agents that never sleep, so things get done the day they land.
- **Control.** Live numbers every day, with every figure traceable to the document behind it.
- **An asset.** Your data and your knowledge layer are yours, and a business that runs this way carries value.
- **And it grows.** The longer it runs, the sharper it gets.

**How we work.** Your tools stay. Each workflow is proven on your own history before it goes live. Nothing high-impact happens without your team's approval. And we run it, always: we monitor it, fix it and keep improving it, so your team never has to maintain anything.

**What running it includes.** Your AI strategy, every month. Training for each person, and for anyone new who joins. All AI and hosting costs. Monitoring and fixes. Changes whenever your process changes. Upgrades as better models come out. Your knowledge layer kept current.

## Rules for the words

- **Buzzwords are in where they carry weight:** AI-native, Command Centre, compound, your private AI knowledge layer. Your own review of the wording catalogue kept lines that sound good in a proposal, and the same applies here.
- **Lead with capacity, not savings,** and follow your wording classes: "only has to check", "never has to maintain", "your", not "the".
- **No client names, figures or trade detail from any proposal.** Those documents are confidential, so every example is written fresh for any business.
- **No invented numbers and no ROI sums.** The value comes through in the day-to-day table.
- **Every screen is labelled as illustrative,** as on the home page.
